
infinibr8kr
===========
by HailTheFish (octojam  7)


About:
------
XO-Chip brick-breaker game with a little twist. Move the paddle to hit the ball and break bricks, but as you 
break bricks, more get added. Don't drop the ball, or let the bricks reach your paddle, or it's game over!


Controls:
---------
"5" and "7" (or left and right arrow keys) to control the paddle.
