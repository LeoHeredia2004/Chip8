
Octo Party Mix!
===============
by Cratmang (Octojam 7)


About:
------
Are you ready to party? The octos sure are! Play through the endless gauntlet of microgames to prove 
what a party animal you are! How high can you score?


Controls:
---------
There are three microgames (I know it doesn't sound like much, but the CHIP-8 is not exactly a beefy console). 
Two of them are played by pressing E. The other one uses WASD or the directional keys. If, by some weird chance, 
you're playing using a COSMAC VIP keypad (you know who you are), use 6 and 5789 respectively.
