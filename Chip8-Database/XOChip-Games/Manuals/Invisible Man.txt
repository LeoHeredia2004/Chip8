
Invisible Man
=============
by MrEmerson (Octojam 7)


About:
------
Recently found  as part of a long-lost archive of early digital black studies at Saint Augustine's University, 
this  game is an adaptation of the prologue of Ralph Ellison's novel Invisible Man.


Controls:
---------
Move the player left (7) and right (9) as he walks down the street.
