
Chicken Scratch
===============
by John Earnest (Octojam 7)


About:
------
You're a chicken. You want to eat bugs. How fast can you get 'em?


Controls:
---------
* Use "5/7/8/9" to run. Press and hold to dash, which is only slightly faster than running.
* Press "6" to peck at bugs. (or dirt. or small rocks. you can peck at anything, really.)
* Press "4" to use echolocation (an ability chickens have) to find distant bugs.
