One day, the Octos were all busy partying, making and playing games for decades-old computers that 
can barely run them. But when the squids came and saw how much fun the octos were having, they grew 
jealous, and kidnapped the Octo Princess! Now a brave octo must find the sacred artifacts from the 
World Above, venture to Castle Daiouika, and defeat the Daiouika Wizard in order to save the Octo 
Princess! Will that brave octo be you?


Controls (Keyboard):
--------------------
[On mobile, tap the screen to bring up the VIP keypad]

WASD/Arrow keys [5789]: Move North, West, South, and East respectively.

Q [4]: Skip turn.

1: Attack (You will be asked to input a direction)!

3: Cancel attack. 

Press E [6] to start the game. 


Inspired by Ultima by Lord British. Created for Octojam 8 using Octo.
