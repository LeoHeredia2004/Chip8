
Into the Garlicscape
====================

"Come, brave adventurer; don your woolen armor and descend into the GarlicScape in search of aliums. But beware
many a moth or roach stands in your way! Take solace in the Llamas. They will heal your wounds, and ensure that 
you have the strength to make it to the fabled Alium Chalice of Yarnham."


Controls:
---------
Move with ASWD or the arrow keys. Dismiss static dialogs by pressing E or the spacebar.
