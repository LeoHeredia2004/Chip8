Draw a single stroke, flip tiles from black to white or vice versa, and turn each single line into a solid 
color each.

A Polarium demake for HP 48 graphing calculators, implemented in Octo for SUPER-CHIP.

Instructions on how to load and run this program on your own HP 48 calculator
Gameplay

Chipolarium is a puzzle game with 100 levels, where the goal for each level is to flip black and white tiles 
so each row is a single color, by drawing a connected stroke through all tiles to flip.
Controls

The controls will vary depending on whether you're running in your browser, on an actual HP 48 calculator, 
or in a general CHIP-8/SUPER-CHIP emulator/interpreter.


	                      | Web / Octo	      | HP 48 	 | CHIP-8  |
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- 
Move	                      | W A S D or arrow keys |	5 1 2 3  | 5 7 8 9 |
Select, draw/finish stroke    | E, Space or Return    | 6 	 | 6       |
Return to menu, cancel stroke | Q or Escape 	      | 4	 | 4       |
Show hint	              | H	              | SPC	 | B       |
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
