Controls:
---------
5 = Draw up
6 = Draw Left
7 = Draw Down
8 = Draw Right

5 & 6 = Draw Diagonaly
5 & 8 = Draw Diagonaly
6 & 7 = Draw Diagonaly
7 & 8 = Draw Diagonaly
