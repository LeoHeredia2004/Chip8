
Worm
====
by rstein (octojam 7)


About:
------
I recently wrote a Chip-8 Interpreter and while researching i stumbled upon the Octojam and decided to 
write a game for it. As i am really creative it is a snake clone. This is supposed to run without waiting 
for vsync after every sprite draw (vblank option in octo), as my interpreter didn't do this when i wrote 
the game.


Controls:
---------
Movement is "5/7/8/9" and to start a new game press "6" at the end screen.
