
Space Defense! - by Jim South (2014)
------------------------------------
Iron Pencil Games


Summary
-------
Defend your planet from alien invaders by blowing them to bits.
Don’t let any ships get past you or it’s game over!


Controls
--------
5 and 8 move up and down, press 6 to fire.
