
Falling - by verisimilitude (2019)
----------------------------------
http://verisimilitudes.net/2019-10-30


A key symbol will appear in a random position at the top of the screen and begin to 
descend. Press the corresponding key to stop it and start anew. Failure to do this 
lets the key hit the bottom and the game then ends.


Controls:
---------
Press the corresponding key (0 - F)
