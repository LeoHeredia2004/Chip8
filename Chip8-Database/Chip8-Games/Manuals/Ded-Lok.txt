
Ded-Lok - by ParityB1t (2016)
-----------------------------

Dodge enemy arrows!

Shooting a lot of arrows creates an 'arrow forest' making it hard to estimate 
enemy position, so be vigilant! ( Doesn't apply to enemy :D )


Controls:
---------
'7' and '9' to move left and right.
'5' to shoot

Press '6' to reset once the game is over.
