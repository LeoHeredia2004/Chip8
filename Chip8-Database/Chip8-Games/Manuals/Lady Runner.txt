
Lady Runner
===========
by noodulz (Octojam 7)


About:
------
A short and simple CHIP-8 and XO-CHIP-supported game consisting of a simple runner. You are a lady knight 
who has to defeat the incoming enemies to reach the princess and live happily ever after. Get touched by 
an enemy and you must start over!


Controls:
---------
Press '6' to attack and to continue the screen. 

New update to come soon to expand the game!
