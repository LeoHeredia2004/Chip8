
Planet of the Eights - by Comrat (2016)
---------------------------------------
OctoJam 3


The last 32-bit register is trying to survive on the terrible planet of the Eights using air balloon.


Controls:
---------
Use 5/7/8/9 (WASD) keys to control air balloon.

If you will reach the earth - i will die
    
Press "8" to start a game.
