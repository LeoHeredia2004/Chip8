
Clostro
=======
by jibbl (Octojam 7)


About:
------
Clostro, a Jezzball clone. Can you make it through all 8 levels and see the ending?


Controls:
---------
"5/7/8/9" = move the crosshair up/down/left/right

"4" = draw a vertical line

"6" = draw a horizontal line

55887979 = Cheat Code (up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right)
