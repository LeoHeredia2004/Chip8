
Enchantment Extra Enhanced by verisimilitude (2020) 
---------------------------------------------------

As this is a game I've written, it's appropriate to explain its history. The intent in 
writing this was to create a small core of gameplay which could be expressed concisely, 
and expanded on later for the next Octo Jam. The player is a lone pixel avoiding diagonal 
lines being drawn, without any end.

Controls:
---------
"8" = Up
"4" = Left
"6" = Right
"2" = Down
