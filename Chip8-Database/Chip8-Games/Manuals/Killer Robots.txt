Killer Robots is an adaptation of the TRS-80 game Robots. 

The player is placed on a 8x8 grid and must avoid getting caught by two 
killer robots. Force beams are positioned on the grid. The player can 
see and try to avoid these, but the robots are oblivious to them and will 
just run into them and get destroyed. Robots can also run into each other, 
in which case one of the robots is destroyed. Once 20 robots have been 
destroyed, the player wins, but if the player moves into a force beam or 
is caught by a robot, the player loses.
