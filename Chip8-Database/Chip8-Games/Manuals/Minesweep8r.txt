
Minesweep8r - by James Kohli aka Hottie Pippen (2014)
-----------------------------------------------------
Octo-Ber Jam 1


Minesweper port for the Chip-8.


Controls:
---------
5/7/8/9 (WASD) to move, 4 (Q) to click, 6 (E) to flag, D (R) to restart.
