
Flappy Pong - by cnelmortimer (2017)
------------------------------------
OctoJam 4


Flappy Pong is a Flappy Bird clone for Chip-8. Players win if they are able to obtain 
255 points (a cup is painted then). This game was created to be shared with the Pin8 
emulator.


Controls:
---------
Press "5" to flap.
