
Grave Digger - by TomR (2017)
-----------------------------
OctoJam 4


In grave digger you pull skeletons from the ground to help their ghosts escape!


Controls:
---------
7 & 9 to move paddle.


P.S. from the author:
---------------------
I stole this game from the first Octo Jam and changed the bricks to ghosts.
