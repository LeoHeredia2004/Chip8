
Fuse - by ??? (unknown author)(xxxx)
------------------------------------

A minimalist reinterpretation of the classic Pipe Dream.


Assemble lines to guide a burning fuse. Don't let it burn out!
You cannot lay down a line overlapping any existing lines.

Controls:
---------
5 = Move Up current line fragments
7 = Move Left current line fragments
8 = Move Down current line fragments
9 = Move Right current line fragments

6 = to lay them down
