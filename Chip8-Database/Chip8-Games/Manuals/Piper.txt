
Piper - by Jordan Mecom, Alice & Lilian Wang (2017)
---------------------------------------------------
Global Game Jam 2017


Play as a small bird to collect goodies and avoid waves


This game is inspired by Pixar's short film of the same name. Piper is a little 
bird who is learning to collect shells and goodies on the beach. But when the 
wave comes in, she needs to hide in the sand to avoid getting wet. The waves 
wash in new goodies for Piper to pick up. Collect as many goodies as you can 
and avoid getting wet! This is a Chip8 game made with Octo. 


Controls:
--------- 
Movement: 5, 7, 8, 9 
Hide: 6
