
Warshaws Revenge - by Ethan Pini (2019)
---------------------------------------

This fun shooter is the antithesis to the great crash of '83.
A "Yar's Revenge" Atari 2600 game clone.


Control (hexpad):
-----------------
5 = go UP  
7 = go LEFT
8 = go DOWN
9 = go RIGHT
B = Shoot



Manual made by Sly DC.
