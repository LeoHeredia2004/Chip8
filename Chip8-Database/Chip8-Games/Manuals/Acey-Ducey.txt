
Acey-Ducey - by Phil Baltzer (1978)
-----------------------------------
ACEY-DUCEY is a card game in which the dealer shows two cards from deck. You bet 
(from 1 to 9) that the next dealer card lies between or equal to the first two 
cards in face value (ACES are low). 

In order to obtain a new deal, press the zero key, and then bet as before. Try for 
a score of 100 or greater. 


Controls:
---------
1 to 9 = Betting
0 = New Deal
