Cosmac Asteroids 
By J.L. Elkhorne (1982)

You have a ship which you can move around the screen and fire 
missiles at huge asteroids which come flying across the screen.
If your missile hits an asteroid, you score. If the asteroid 
hits you, points are deducted from your score. Your score is 
displayed at the end of the game and a reset brings a new 
asteroid on screen.

Keys:
-----
2 = UP
4 = LEFT
6 = RIGHT
8 = DOWN
5 = FIRE MISSILE
