
Wipe Off - by Joseph Weisbecker (19xx)
--------------------------------------
Cosmac VIP CDP18S711 Book - Page 42 (VIP-311)


This program uses the CHIP-8 INTERPRETER at 0000-01FF Serve the ball by 
pressing any key. Move the paddle left or right by pressing key 4 or 6. 

Try to wipe out as many spots as possible. Each spot counts one point. 
You get 20 balls. You see your final score at the end of the game. You can 
make the paddle wider by changing the E0 byte at 02CD to F8 or FF.
