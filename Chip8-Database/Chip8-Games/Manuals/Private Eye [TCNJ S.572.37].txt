NOTE: Was this made by Joyce Weisbecker because it looks very much as "Sequence Shoot".
       

This one is a simple shooter. Just try to shoot the big moving white square.
If you are successful to shot it, you get 10 points.


Controls:
---------
3 = Shoot Upward
6 = Shoot Strait
9 = Shoot Downward
