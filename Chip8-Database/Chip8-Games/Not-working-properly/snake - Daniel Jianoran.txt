
Snake - by Daniel Jianoran (2018)
---------------------------------

It is a small version of Snake. There is no wall so you
can go to the other side of the screen without dying.
Once you touch an apple (dot), you will hear a beep and then
go on to the next apple. If you touch your tail, the game end.

Control (hexpad):
-----------------
5 to go UP
7 to go LEFT
8 to go DOWN
9 to go RIGHT
