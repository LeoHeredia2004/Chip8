
Knockout - by Steve Houk (1980)
-------------------------------
Cosmac VIP Game II VP-720 Book - Page 21


The object of thei game is to knock out the blocks in the solid wall. 
There are  1280 blocks in this wall and they are knocked out by firing 
a ball at the wall. The paddle below is used to rebound the ball to keep 
it in play longer.


How to Play:
------------
1. Load the Chip-8X interpreter at 0000 - 02FF and the game starting at
   0300.

2. Flip the reset-rn switch to run.

3. The paddle can be moved from right to left by pressing the keys 4 and 6.
   Key 5 fires the ball. Each block is worth 1 point and you are given 20 balls.


NOTE: This program will run on an unmodified black and white VIP without color.

      Also, a Simple Sound VP-595 board, if used, will provide different tone
      beeps with each knockout.
