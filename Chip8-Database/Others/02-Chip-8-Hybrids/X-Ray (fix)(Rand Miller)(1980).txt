X-Ray
-----
Programmed by: Rand Miller, RCA COSMAC Game Manual II, VP-720
3K memory minimum


X-Ray is a game where targets are hidden within an 8x8 matrix,
You are to locate these targets by sending rays in and observing where
they emerge. The game board is shown below:

  
     1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8 
 20                         9
 1F                         A
 1E                         B
 1D                         C
 1C                         D
 1B                         E
 1A                         F
 19                         10
    18 17 16 15 14 13 12 11

The numbers around the edges are used to specify where the rays are
sent in or where they come out. The game can be played with 4 or 5 
targets.



To play:
--------
1. Select number of targets to be hidden (4 or 5). This number will be
   displayed within the box after step 2.

2. Select number of rounds to be played. (1 - F)

3. Enter nuber where to send ray IN. This is a two digit number. For
   example, a ray to be sent in at B would be entered as 0B. If the ray
   number is greater than 20, step 3 will be repeated.


To know more to how to play this game, check out the VP-720 Cosmac Game
manual vol.2, thanks.
