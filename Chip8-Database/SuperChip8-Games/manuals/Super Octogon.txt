A simple lo-fi action game for Super CHIP-8, featuring twice as many edges and angles as Super Square. 
Move your pixel with A/D or the Left and Right cursor keys, but don't touch the sides!
About

This game was developed as part of  Octojam 8, using Octo. Its source and assets are all made available 
under a Creative Commons Zero "No Rights Reserved" license. This game should work on any modern SCHIP 
interpreter, with one important caveat: it relies upon drawing 16x16 sprites while in 64x32 "low-resolution" 
display mode; as a consequence it will not work on an HP-48 version of SCHIP.
