
Turm8
=====
by Tobias V. Langhoff (octojam 7)


About:
------
This is a collection of cellular automata called "turmites", implemented in Octo for SUPER-CHIP on the 
HP 48 graphing calculators.

A "turmite" (also known as "TurNing machine") is a type of two-dimensional Turing machine. It consists 
of an ant that moves over a grid of pixels. Depending on the current pixel's color (limited to two colors 
in this implementation) and the ant's internal state, it can rotate, change its state, and/or change the 
color of the pixel before moving. From these simple rules, interesting patterns and behaviors emerge.


Controls:
---------
* "5/7/8/9" to move in menu.
* "B" = Select
* "F" and "E" = adjust speed
* "A" = return to menu
