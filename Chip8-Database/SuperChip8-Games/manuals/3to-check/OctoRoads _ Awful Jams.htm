OctoRoads

Team: The Bobcats

Source

Isn't it time for a 3D, third-person perspective game for Octo? Pilot your spaceship along a treacherous road. It has holes in the most inconvenient places! Keep your speed up or your jumps will fall short of their targets.

There are 4 levels.

This is a trimmed-down clone of SkyRoads.

Keys:

  • W - accelerate
  • S - decelerate
  • A - move left
  • D - move right
  • C - jump
